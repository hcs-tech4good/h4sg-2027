Your field guide / FAQs
What is a hackathon?+

A weekend where students come together to code, build, and turn ideas into implementable projects.

What is unique about Hack 4 Social Good?+

Hack 4 Social Good focuses on impact: teams work on real social challenges, including problems brought forward by nonprofit and community partners.

Who can participate?+

Hack 4 Social Good is open to undergraduate students from any school, major, and experience level.

What can I win?+

We offer one prize per track. You can also win prizes through mini-events and raffles throughout the weekend. You'll get free swag just by submitting a project!

Do I need coding experience?+

Not at all! All weekend, there will be beginner-friendly workshops covering the basics and mentors on hand.

How much does it cost?+

The event is free for accepted hackers, including meals, workshops, mentorship, and event programming.

How big can teams be?+

Teams can be a max of 4 people.

What if I don't have a team?+

You will have opportunities to find people to form a team through the Discord and/or at the hackathon!

What will we do other than hack?+

There will be professional opportunities, including coffee chats; workshops for learning different skills; and fun, team-building activities!
